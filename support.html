CityFeel 是一款伦敦街区感受应用：在地图上查看 40 个片区的氛围画像、关键词和主色调，点进片区可以看白天和夜晚两个版本。当前版本为只读展示，片区数据标注为“初始判断”。

Support

CityFeel · Developer: WEIWEI SONG

CityFeel is an app about how London neighbourhoods feel: view 40 areas on a map, each with an atmosphere portrait, keywords and a main colour. Tap an area to see its day and night versions. The current version is read-only, and area data is labelled as an initial judgement.

How to use

Tap an area on the map or pick one from the list to see its portrait and keywords. Use the day / night switch at the top to see both versions.

Beta

The current version is distributed for testing through TestFlight. If it will not install, check that CityFeel 1.0 appears in TestFlight and reinstall; if the problem persists, contact us.
