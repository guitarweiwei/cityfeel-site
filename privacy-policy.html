隐私政策

CityFeel（城感） · 开发者：WEIWEI SONG · 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 15 日

CityFeel 是一款伦敦街区感受应用：40 个伦敦片区，每个片区一张 AI 生成的氛围画像、三个关键词、一个主色调，分白天和夜晚。打开应用即可看到你所在片区的感受，并可以提交你对所在片区的感受。片区数据标注为“初始判断”。

我们收集什么数据

我们不收集任何数据。本应用没有账号、没有登录、没有广告、没有第三方 SDK、没有数据分析、没有后端服务器，也不会将任何信息上传到任何服务器。

位置信息

应用在使用期间可能会请求定位权限，仅用于找到离你最近的片区、显示该片区的感受。定位只在你的手机上使用，不会被收集、存储或分享，也不会离开设备。拒绝定位权限不影响浏览全部 40 个片区，你也可以手动选择片区。

本机保存的内容

你提交的感受（最多三个词、四个滑杆、提交时间，以及是否是在所在片区提交的标记）只保存在你的设备上，不会上传。画像和片区数据随应用安装，不包含任何用户信息。

感受提交

你可以提交对片区的感受（最多三个词、四个滑杆，不用打字、不用注册）。提交按片区和时段汇总，不包含精确定位，只记录是否在某个片区内。每个片区每个时段 24 小时内只能提交一次。

AI 画像

氛围画像为 AI 生成的插画，随应用内置，不含真实人物、真实地标、文字和用户内容。

儿童

本应用不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童的数据，也没有面向儿童的账号或聊天功能。

联系我们

如对本隐私政策或应用有任何问题，请联系：cornerstories.support@gmail.com。

政策更新

如本政策有更新，我们会在本页面更新生效日期。应用本身不收集数据，因此政策更新不会改变任何数据处理方式。

Privacy Policy

CityFeel · Developer: WEIWEI SONG · Effective: 15 November 2026

CityFeel is an app about how London neighbourhoods feel: 40 areas, each with one AI-generated atmosphere portrait, three keywords and a main colour, for day and night. Opening the app shows how the area around you feels, and you can submit how an area feels to you. Area data is labelled as an initial judgement.

What data we collect

We collect no data. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no third-party SDKs, no analytics and no backend server, and nothing is uploaded to any server.

Location

The app may ask for your location while you use it, only to find the nearest area and show how it feels. Your location is used on this phone only and is never collected, stored or shared — it never leaves the device. Declining location permission does not affect browsing all 40 areas; you can also pick an area yourself.

What is stored on your device

Feels you submit (up to three words, four sliders, the time of submission, and a flag for whether it was submitted while at the area) are stored on your device only and are never uploaded. Portraits and area data are bundled with the app and contain no user information.

Feel submissions

You can submit how an area feels (up to three words and four sliders, no typing, no account). Submissions are aggregated by area and time of day and never include precise location — only whether you are inside an area. Each area can receive one submission per time of day within 24 hours.

AI portraits

Atmosphere portraits are AI-generated illustrations bundled with the app. They contain no real people, no real landmarks, no text and no user content.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children, and has no accounts or chat features for children.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or the app, contact us at cornerstories.support@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date on this page. Because the app collects no data, a policy change does not change how any data is handled.
