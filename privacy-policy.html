隐私政策

CityFeel（城感） · 开发者：WEIWEI SONG · 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 14 日

CityFeel 是一款伦敦街区感受应用：40 个伦敦片区，每个片区一张 AI 生成的氛围画像、三个关键词、一个主色调，分白天和夜晚。当前版本为只读展示，片区数据标注为“初始判断”。

我们收集什么数据

我们不收集任何数据。本应用没有账号、没有登录、没有广告、没有第三方 SDK、没有数据分析、没有后端服务器，也不会将任何信息上传到任何服务器。

位置信息

当前版本不请求定位权限，也不收集位置信息。“你在哪里”按钮只会滚动到片区列表，不使用定位。

本机保存的内容

当前版本不在设备上保存任何个人内容。画像和片区数据随应用安装，不包含任何用户信息。

感受提交（未来版本）

未来版本会让你提交对片区的感受（最多三个词、四个滑杆，不用打字、不用注册）。提交按片区和时段汇总，不包含精确定位，只记录是否在某个片区内。该功能上线前，我们会更新本政策。

AI 画像

氛围画像为 AI 生成的插画，随应用内置，不含真实人物、真实地标、文字和用户内容。

儿童

本应用不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童的数据，也没有面向儿童的账号或聊天功能。

联系我们

如对本隐私政策或应用有任何问题，请联系：cornerstories.support@gmail.com。

政策更新

如本政策有更新，我们会在本页面更新生效日期。应用本身不收集数据，因此政策更新不会改变任何数据处理方式。

Privacy Policy

CityFeel · Developer: WEIWEI SONG · Effective: 14 November 2026

CityFeel is an app about how London neighbourhoods feel: 40 areas, each with one AI-generated atmosphere portrait, three keywords and a main colour, for day and night. The current version is read-only, and area data is labelled as an initial judgement.

What data we collect

We collect no data. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no third-party SDKs, no analytics and no backend server, and nothing is uploaded to any server.

Location

The current version does not request location permission and collects no location data. The "How does it feel where you are?" button only scrolls to the area list; it does not use your location.

What is stored on your device

The current version stores no personal content on your device. Portraits and area data are bundled with the app and contain no user information.

Feel submissions (future versions)

A future version will let you submit how an area feels (up to three words and four sliders, no typing, no account). Submissions are aggregated by area and time of day and never include precise location — only whether you are inside an area. This policy will be updated before that feature ships.

AI portraits

Atmosphere portraits are AI-generated illustrations bundled with the app. They contain no real people, no real landmarks, no text and no user content.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children, and has no accounts or chat features for children.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or the app, contact us at cornerstories.support@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date on this page. Because the app collects no data, a policy change does not change how any data is handled.
